For a moment, Fan Changyu couldn't quite see what was crucial here. She asked, “Why do you say that?”

Yu Qianqian explained, “If the county magistrate were lining his own pockets by squeezing the people, then if it were the people above him who wanted a share of the spoils, there would be no need for the magistrate to collude with bandits and blockade all the roads leading to Jizhou Prefectural Office. Even if the matter blew up, the people above him could easily suppress it.

“As for using the old man's death to put pressure on me, it's simply because they've set their sights on Yixiang Restaurant's wealth. Among the wealthy merchants in this town, my foundation is the shallowest, which makes me the easiest to deal with. Normally, I give those officials enough benefits that they'll lend me a hand when I'm faced with some ordinary difficulty. But when something like this happens, naturally, none of them would dare wade into these muddy waters. Once Yixiang Restaurant falls, the county magistrate can go after the other wealthy merchants one by one and squeeze them for silver. If they don't want to end up like me, losing everything and facing imprisonment on top of it, they'll have no choice but to obediently hand over their money.”

Fan Changyu immediately understood the implications. She slapped the table. “Then we need to band together with the other wealthy merchants in Qingping County as quickly as possible!”

Yu Qianqian shook her head. “We need to think this through carefully. I'm only a merchant, so I have no idea how much grain the prefectural government ordered each household to provide. If you hadn't told me that the county magistrate was demanding such an enormous amount of grain, and that he was probably lining his own pockets, I wouldn't have thought this far ahead. After all, if he were merely bullying me, a merchant, the imperial court might not pursue the matter too seriously. But if he's preying on the people of the entire Qingping County, without anyone above him to shield him, he'll be doomed the moment this comes to light. The county magistrate can't possibly fail to understand that. After turning it over in my mind, I can think of only one possibility: he intends to defect to the rebel prince.”

She paused at this point and looked at Fan Changyu. “Only those officials know how much grain the army is supposed to requisition. Changyu **Meizi**<sup class="footnote-ref" data-footnote="younger sister"></sup>, how did you know the county magistrate was demanding more military grain than he was supposed to?”

Fan Changyu repeated the explanation Xie Zheng had given her, then added, “And the county magistrate blocking the road to Jizhou Prefectural Office is surely proof that he's got a guilty conscience!”

Yu Qianqian pondered for a moment. “With the blockade of the prefectural road added to the matter, we can basically conclude that the county magistrate has rebellious intentions. But so long as he refuses to admit it and simply claims that bandits have been robbing travelers along the road, we have no evidence proving that he is in league with them. Without such evidence, we can't convince the common people.

“The only thing that can truly point to the county magistrate is that grain requisition order. But simply saying that this year's requisition is greater than in previous years can't serve as evidence against him. The government of Jizhou wouldn't ordinarily drive the people to such extremes. Besides, Huizhou has just suffered a defeat, and the grain routes have been disrupted. No one knows what those officials might be thinking.”

After hearing Xie Zheng's analysis, Fan Changyu had thought his words made perfect sense. Yet now, listening to Yu Qianqian, she suddenly felt that Yu Qianqian's reasoning was just as sound.

She carefully considered what Xie Zheng and Yu Qianqian had said, and a few strange feelings suddenly stirred in the depths of her mind.

Yu Qianqian had said that only the officials knew how much grain the army was supposed to requisition. Yet when Yan Zheng had made those remarks before, he had sounded as though he knew exactly how much grain the government was supposed to requisition.

And there was something else. Yu Qianqian was worried that, for the sake of the war, the officials of Jizhou Prefectural Office might disregard the lives of the people below them. Yet Yan Zheng had been absolutely certain that the officials of Jizhou Prefectural Office would never drive the people to such extremes.

Was it because Yu Qianqian had been doing business for many years and considered every matter more thoroughly, while Yan Zheng simply lacked her worldly experience and therefore held the government to such high expectations?

Yet judging from the facts before them, Yan Zheng seemed to have been right after all. The amount of grain Jizhou Prefectural Office had actually ordered requisitioned was nowhere near as much as the county magistrate had collected.

She frowned as she mulled these things over, so tangled in thought that her eyebrows were practically fighting with each other.

Seeing her expression, Yu Qianqian assumed she was fretting over how to expose the county magistrate and comforted her, “Changyu **Meizi**, don't worry yourself over me. I'll slowly find a way to deal with Yixiang Restaurant's troubles myself.”

Given the county magistrate's current efforts to smear Yixiang Restaurant by using the old man who had died after suffering an attack of *sheep-horned-wind*, it was obvious that he intended to have Yu Qianqian thrown into prison.

Fan Changyu pressed her lips together. “That Advisor He is the county magistrate's lackey. He was also the one who hired people to smear you. I reckon there's an eighty percent chance that Advisor He knows how much military grain the Jizhou Prefectural Office actually ordered. What if we start with him?”

Yu Qianqian looked puzzled. “How?”

Fan Changyu said, “Knock him unconscious with a stick, put him in a sack, drag him back to the tower, and interrogate him. What do you think?”

Yu Qianqian stared at the seemingly honest, sweet-faced young woman before her, finding it hard to believe that such words had actually come from her mouth.

Then she remembered that Fan Changyu had just used the exact same method to deal with the few ruffians on her behalf, and the astonishment in her heart eased somewhat.

After carefully considering it, she nodded. “Very well. At this point, we might as well *treat a dead horse as though it were still alive*<sup class="footnote-ref" data-footnote="an idiom meaning that when all conventional options seem exhausted, one might as well try a desperate remedy."></sup>.”

She looked at Fan Changyu. “But you stay out of this. I'll hire someone to do it. Right now, the county magistrate's main targets for extortion are Qingping County's wealthy merchants. If this comes to light and the investigation traces back to you, your husband and your younger sister will be dragged into it as well.”

The retort that had risen to Fan Changyu's lips died there, swallowed by the latter half of Yu Qianqian's words.

On her own, Fan Changyu was indeed fearless. But the moment Changning was involved, she no longer dared act recklessly.

If anything happened to her, Changning would be left with no one in this world to rely on.

And there was Yan Zheng, too. He was about to leave soon, and she couldn't let him get dragged into a lawsuit like this because of her either.

In the end, Fan Changyu could only ask, “Then, **Zhangguide**<sup class="footnote-ref" data-footnote="another form to address a manager/shopkeeper"></sup>, is there anything else I can help with?”

Yu Qianqian smiled at her. “You've already helped me so much by coming up with ideas. Doesn't that count as helping? And don't keep calling me **Zhangguide** from now on. It makes us sound so distant. I'm six or seven years older than you, but don't make me sound old, either. Just call me Qianqian.”

Fan Changyu could clearly feel that Yu Qianqian was treating her with greater familiarity than before. She didn't dwell on the matter of how to address her and immediately said, “Qianqian.”

Yu Qianqian's eyes curved into a smile. Then she said, “I heard you brought over a cartload of braised meat. There's no way I can sell it here today. It would be a shame for you to take it home and have it spoil before you can sell it all. How about this? Take it to Zuili Restaurant. I'm on good terms with the owner, and he happens to have a banquet today. Your braised meat will come in handy.”

As she spoke, she called over one of the workers in the tower and had him accompany Fan Changyu to Zuili Restaurant. She also settled the bill for the braised meat with Fan Changyu. “I'll pay you first on behalf of the owner of Zuili Restaurant. I'll collect the money from him later.”

Fan Changyu refused. “You should deal with the troubles at the tower first. There’s no rush to pay me; you can hand over the money once the Zuili Restaurant has paid you."

Yu Qianqian placed the money pouch in her hand. “Take it. If Yixiang Restaurant still can't be saved, I might have to bring Bao'er over to your place and freeload for a couple of days. I can't be owing you money on top of that.”

With Yu Qianqian putting it that way, Fan Changyu could only accept the money.

After delivering the braised meat to Zuili Restaurant, Fan Changyu had the old man driving the cart take the Yixiang Restaurant worker back to the tower. After thinking it over, she decided to pay a visit to Head Constable Wang's home.

It wasn't because she wanted Head Constable Wang to help Yu Qianqian. Fan Changyu knew that Head Constable Wang was an upright man. If the county magistrate ordered him and the constables under him to do something vile, and Head Constable Wang, unaware that the county magistrate had rebellious intentions, unknowingly became the magistrate's blade, he would likely spend the rest of his life tormented by guilt.

And if he were branded a rebel on top of that, it would be an even greater loss than it was worth.

Fan Changyu knocked on Head Constable Wang's door. The same old woman opened it, but unlike before, she was no longer delighted to see Fan Changyu. Instead, she wore a worried expression. “It's Fan **Niangzi**<sup class="footnote-ref" data-footnote="Lady"></sup>.”

Fan Changyu asked, “Is Constable Wang home?”

The woman replied, “Yes.”

The old woman ushered her inside. Head Constable Wang and his wife were both in the main room. Perhaps it was only Fan Changyu's imagination, but she couldn't help feeling that there was a certain air of dejection about Head Constable Wang. He seemed less spirited than before.

Wang Furen<sup class="footnote-ref" data-footnote="Furen means Madam"></sup>, however, smiled and invited her to sit. “Changyu, you've come. Have you run into some trouble?”

Fan Changyu shook her head. “No. I came to ask Wang **Shu**<sup class="footnote-ref" data-footnote="uncle"></sup> about something concerning the county yamen.”

Wang Furen's face immediately showed some hesitation. “Your Wang **Shu**... I'm afraid he can't help you with much. He isn't working at the county yamen anymore.”

Fan Changyu exclaimed in surprise, “Since when?”

Wang Furen sighed. “On the second day of the New Year, your **Shu** went to the county yamen for duty, only to be told that he didn't need to come back anymore. They said he hadn't done a good enough job investigating the treasure-map murder case. All the young constables who worked under your **Shu** were replaced as well. That county magistrate usually keeps such a low profile, yet just as he's about to be transferred, he pulls something like this.”

Fan Changyu's eyelid twitched at that. Clearly, the county magistrate knew what kind of man Head Constable Wang was and had deliberately removed him in advance for fear that he would get in the way.

She asked, “Does Wang **Shu** know where the constables currently working at the county yamen came from?”

Head Constable Wang shook his head. “I didn't ask. But I heard they were specially recruited from among people with decent martial skills.”

Fan Changyu pressed her lips together. “They may be bandits.”

Both Head Constable Wang and his wife froze.

Wang Furen was the first to recover. With an awkward smile, she said, “Child, what nonsense are you talking about?”

Fan Changyu's expression was grave. “**Shenshen**<sup class="footnote-ref" data-footnote="auntie"></sup>, I'm not talking nonsense. The county magistrate has colluded with the mountain bandits to blockade the road to the Prefectural Office. He's also using the requisition of military grain as an excuse to squeeze the people for their wealth. Now he's setting his sights on all the wealthy merchants in Qingping County. He's clearly planning to take all this money and defect to the rebel prince.”

The moment she finished speaking, the entire room fell so silent that even the sound of a falling needle could be heard.

Head Constable Wang remained stunned for a long while, unable to recover from the shock. At last, he could only murmur, “This... How could this be possible?”

Fan Changyu then told him about Yu Qianqian's ordeal. “He's no longer simply forcing the owner of Yixiang Restaurant to hand over money. He wants everything Yixiang Restaurant has. And he's blockaded the road, cutting off the route from the county seat to Jizhou Prefectural Office.”

Head Constable Wang had already begun to believe her, but the news was simply too shocking to take in. He was still trying to convince himself otherwise. “Could the blockade of the road simply be to stop Yu **Zhanggui** from going to the Prefectural Office to lodge a complaint against him?”

Seeing this, Fan Changyu also realized that Yu Qianqian's concerns were not unfounded. Without solid evidence, when all one had were scattered clues and suspicions, most people—even if they harbored doubts—wouldn't dare take sides without seeing irrefutable proof.

She remembered what Yu Qianqian had said about the grain requisition order and asked, “Wang **Shu**, have you ever seen the grain requisition order that the Jizhou Prefectural Office sent to Qingping County? If the amount of military grain the county magistrate is requisitioning doesn't match what's written in the order, then that would be irrefutable evidence.”

Head Constable Wang shook his head. “How would I ever get to see that? The county magistrate and Advisor He are the ones who look it over, then they directly instruct the men below to collect the grain. But all the official documents are kept in the county yamen's archives. I'm still on fairly good terms with the clerk in charge of the records. He should be able to see the grain requisition order.”

Fan Changyu's heart began pounding, and she unconsciously felt a layer of sweat gather in her palms. “Can we go find that Clerk Liu?”

Head Constable Wang had spent many years investigating cases, and his temperament was steady and composed. “We can't alert the enemy. I was just dismissed from my post, and if I immediately go to Clerk Liu's home afterward, then if the county magistrate truly does have rebellious intentions, I'm afraid someone will report my visit to him the moment I set foot in Clerk Liu's home.”

Wang Furen suddenly said, “We haven't gone to the Liu family to pay our New Year's respects yet, right? There, now we have a perfectly good reason! It's almost noon anyway. **Lao Tuozi**<sup class="footnote-ref" data-footnote="lit. means Old Man, but here it is an affectionate term for a woman to address her husband (usually by elderly couple)."></sup>, you stay home. I'll take Changyu to the Liu Residence with some New Year's gifts. Surely the county magistrate's people won't notice anything amiss.”

Head Constable Wang nodded. “That'll work.”

Wang Furen picked out several New Year's gifts and took Fan Changyu to Clerk Liu's home.

After they explained the reason for their visit, Clerk Liu was also taken aback. He then said, “Jizhou Prefectural Office indeed sent over documents concerning the requisition of military grain. However, I've never seen those documents myself. The grain requisition order has always been kept with the county magistrate.”

The county magistrate had consistently held on to the documents instead of handing them over to Clerk Liu for filing. This was yet another confirmation of his rebellious intentions.

Fan Changyu and Wang Furen left the Liu Residence, both walking back with heavy hearts.

Without the grain requisition order, their only remaining hope lay with Advisor He.

Wang Furen said sorrowfully, “That wretched county magistrate is taking the money and grain to join the rebel prince. What are those farmers supposed to live on now that all their seed grain has been looted?”

Fan Changyu glanced at the sun, wondering whether Yu Qianqian had managed to capture Advisor He with her people.

The moment that thought crossed her mind, another idea suddenly flashed through her head.

She turned to Wang Furen and said, “**Shenshen**, why don't we just kidnap the county magistrate himself?”

The redness in Wang Furen's eyes had yet to fade. She stared at the sweet, well-behaved young woman before her, opened her mouth, and remained speechless for a long while.
