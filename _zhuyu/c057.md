The hot wind, heavy with the stench of burning, melted the snowflakes that had yet to fall from the night sky.

A barefoot child came shrieking out of a house engulfed in roaring flames, only to be cut down by a bandit after taking no more than two steps through the snow. The blood that burst from the child's body splattered across the hooves of the horse beneath Sui Yuanqing.

Turning a dagger that gleamed coldly in his hand, he looked down at Magistrate Liu from horseback. “Wasn't it you who orchestrated the scheme to have me bound and handed over, all to quell the people's anger and claim credit for restoring justice? How many people I lose in Qingping County that day? Tonight, I'll collect a hundredfold—no, a thousandfold in return.”

He tapped Magistrate Liu's cheek with the dagger and sneered, “You claim not to know the identity of the man in the green ghost mask. And you don't know the identity of the maid in your own residence who tied me up either?”

The blade suddenly plunged downward, slicing open a gash in Magistrate Liu's body. He immediately let out a squeal like a slaughtered pig.

Sui Yuanqing drawled, “Know now?”

In his greed to claim credit, Magistrate Liu had publicly declared after suppressing the Qingping County uprising that he and his subordinates had devised the scheme to capture Sui Yuanqing and his party. Only now did he realize what fear truly was.

His fat face trembled as he wept and sniffled uncontrollably, “**Xiaoren**<sup class="footnote-ref" data-footnote="lit. this small/humble person"></sup> truly doesn't know where that woman lives! She isn't a maid from my residence. All **Xiaoren** knows is that her name is Fan Changyu, a butcher's daughter from Lin'an Town. I beg **Shi**—”

A scar-faced man among the bandits rode over. Sui Yuanqing's expression changed. The instant Magistrate Liu blurted out the word “**Shizi**”, Sui Yuanqing drew his blade and slit his throat, tossing the man from the saddle.

The scar-faced man had heard that Sui Yuanqing was looking for a woman. He approached with evident displeasure. “**Wu Di**<sup class="footnote-ref" data-footnote="五弟 - literally Fifth Brother"></sup>, didn't you say you were looking for the enemy who injured you that day? Why are you suddenly asking about a maid from his residence?”

Sui Yuanqing curved his lips into a smile, “That enemy of mine is a maid from this magistrate's residence.”

The scar-faced man's expression relaxed at once. Yet his next words carried an unmistakable warning. “With Shishan Niang's temperament, if you've got some *sweetheart* waiting for you outside, she'll never let her live.”
<div class="author-note">
  <p>红粉知己(Hóngfěn zhījǐ) - refers to a female confidante or a woman with whom a man shares an intimate understanding. In this context, the scar-faced bandit uses it suggestively to mean a romantic interest or lover.</p>
  <p>The Name Shishan Niang (十三娘) lit. means Thirteenth Lady</p>
</div>

Sui Yuanqing's smile did not reach his eyes. “**Da Ge**<sup class="footnote-ref" data-footnote="lit. Eldest Brother; usually a term to call the big boss of a gang"></sup> jests. I nearly ended up at the bottom of the river because of that woman. This is a debt I must settle, no matter what.”

The scar-faced man looked at the magistrate's corpse sprawled on the ground. “The brothers already stripped the magistrate's residence bare. We didn't see a single martial maidservant. You rode more than a dozen li<sup class="footnote-ref" data-footnote="1 Li (里) = 0.5 km"></sup> after that woman yourself and still couldn't find her. What, can she fly through the heavens and burrow into the earth?”

As he spoke, he turned toward Sui Yuanqing, “We've plundered Qingping County clean. The troops from Jizhou will surely come hunting us down. We need to get back to Qingfeng Stronghold<sup class="footnote-ref" data-footnote="Zhai (寨) refers to a fortified mountain settlement or bandit stronghold."></sup> as soon as possible.”

The implication was clear: he did not want Sui Yuanqing continuing his search for that woman.

Sui Yuanqing tugged on the reins, restraining the restless steed beneath him. A faint smile played at the corners of his lips. “I’ll follow what **Da Ge** say.”

Seeing that he truly had abandoned the search for the woman who had wounded him, the scar-faced man was somewhat appeased. He shouted, “Once we've finished looting this town, we're heading back to Qingfeng Stronghold!”

With a long spear hanging from his saddle, Sui Yuanqing tugged the reins and followed at a leisurely pace, as though strolling through a garden. When townspeople in tattered clothes darted out from dark alleyways, he did not so much as blink before his spear flashed, flinging up a spray of crimson blood.

The scar-faced man seemed to finally set his mind at ease and rode off to inspect another area.

Sui Yuanqing, however, kept one hand around his long spear and lowered his head slightly from atop his horse to ask the man he had just struck down, “Fan Changyu. Where does she live?”

As the name slipped from between his lips, a trace of amused interest appeared at the corners of his mouth, tinged with something predatory and destructive.

If he found that woman, he would likely find the man wearing the green ghost mask as well.

The wounded man on the ground was still gushing blood from his chest. The instinct to survive compelled him to raise a trembling hand and point in one direction, “W-West… west (side of) town…”

Sui Yuanqing squeezed his horse's flanks and rode toward the western part of town amid firelight and falling snow.

The wound at his waist, not yet fully healed, throbbed with faint, intermittent pain from the horse's jolting gait. It did nothing to diminish the pleasure surging through him.

Lin'an Town could not compare to the prosperity of Qingping County. Having just feasted on “fat meat” on the riches of Qingping, the bandits found little interest in Lin'an's “lean bone”. They largely targeted wealthy households, as poorer families were particularly troublesome to rob.

Most of the time, they found no silver at all. They had to threaten those hiding inside with their blades before the residents would dig out the money they had carefully stashed away in the most obscure corners.

When Sui Yuanqing rode into the alley where Fan Changyu's home stood, he saw numerous bodies strewn across the lane—old people and children alike. In one hand, he held a bloodied man he had seized along the way.

Perhaps someone had already beaten him to the prey he sought. His eyes narrowed. His tone remained languid, but a chill entered his voice. “Which house belongs to Fan Changyu?”

The blood-covered man dangling from his grasp trembled and pointed toward the second house from the end of the alley.

Sui Yuanqing flung him aside. Thinking he had somehow escaped with his life, the man scrambled away on all fours. But the moment he got to his feet, a blade pierced through his chest. He collapsed stiffly, adding another corpse to the ground.

Sui Yuanqing glanced at the blood staining the knife he had picked up. With a look of disgust, he pulled out a handkerchief and wiped his fingers clean. Only then did he step through the Fan family's gate.

Fan Changyu's home had clearly already been ransacked. The courtyard was in disarray, and the main room stood wide open, showing no sign that anyone might be hiding inside.

Sui Yuanqing, however, showed no intention of leaving. He raised his torch and headed inside.

Every drawer and cabinet had been thrown open. Bottles and jars lay shattered across the floor, while the bedsheets and quilts had been dragged onto the ground. Clearly, someone had done this while searching for hidden silver.

He was about to leave when he noticed what appeared to be another door behind the kitchen. After a moment's hesitation, he nonetheless carried his torch over.

Beyond the door lay the Fan family's rear courtyard: a pigsty, a well covered by a heavy stone slab, and a stone bench used specifically for slaughtering pigs.

Sui Yuanqing swept his gaze across the yard. He had already begun to turn away when his eyes fell upon the stone slab covering the dry well.

He stopped.

By the torchlight, he could clearly see that the slab was covered with roughly an inch of accumulated snow. Yet the ground beneath the well's rim, shielded by the slab, was blanketed in a thick layer of snow as well.

Clearly, the slab had been placed there afterward.

Why cover a well with a stone slab?

Naturally, because something was hidden at the bottom.

The slab appeared to weigh over a hundred jin<sup class="footnote-ref" data-footnote="1 jin = 500 g"></sup>. Anyone else would have taken it for an abandoned dry well and walked away without a second thought.

Sui Yuanqing, however, began to laugh softly. His eyes curved into lovely crescent moons, as though he were in rather good spirits.

Holding the torch aloft, he walked step by step toward the well covered by the stone slab. The snow beneath his feet gave clear, crisp cracks with every step. Mingling with the distant cries and wails, the sound seemed almost like fingers plucking at someone's heartstrings.

As he reached up to lift the stone slab, a murderous intent came rushing at him from behind.

Sui Yuanqing hastily dodged to the side. A boning knife skimmed past his ear and embedded itself in the courtyard wall not far away.

Before Sui Yuanqing could even look up toward where the knife had come from, the figure hiding on the roof had already leapt down like a nimble leopard, the bloodletting knife in her hand once more aimed straight at his vitals.

Sui Yuanqing dodged on instinct, but his opponent wielded two blades. One slashed across, followed by another sweeping down from the side, leaving him no room to catch his breath.

Fan Changyu was truly out for blood this time. Changning, Auntie Zhao, and several neighboring households were hidden inside that dry well. If she lost, they would all die without a doubt.

Her attacks were swift and ferocious. In her left hand was a bone-cleaving knife; in her right, a bloodletting knife that could be used to chop like a cleaver or thrust like a sword. She could hardly be said to use any particular technique, seeking only speed as she pressed her opponent into a forced defense, leaving him no opportunity to counterattack.

Several times, Sui Yuanqing tried to draw his sword, but Fan Changyu's relentless blade work forced him to abandon the attempt. He kept retreating, evading the edges of her blades as they bore down on him step after step.

In the end, he simply used the torch in his hand as a weapon. He whirled it through the air with a fierce whoosh, its flames trailing like a strip of orange-yellow silk. But Fan Changyu's assault did not let up, driving him into a position where he could do nothing but defend.

Sui Yuanqing did not dare meet the butcher's knife in her hand head-on with the torch. He could only strike against the back of her blade or use the torch to block while avoiding its edge.

This made every parry extraordinarily difficult. His opponent seemed tireless. Not only were her attacks fierce and swift, but even after fighting for so long, she had not lost the slightest bit of strength. The web between his thumb and forefinger had been battered raw, splitting open from the impacts, and the hand gripping the torch kept going numb in waves.

Sui Yuanqing cursed inwardly. What kind of monster was this woman?

In the flickering light and shadow, Fan Changyu's eyes were cold and keen, like a tiger or leopard tearing into an intruder. They held a merciless ferocity that would not stop until one of them was dead, utterly unlike the guileless, simple demeanor she had shown when at first.

When she let herself go like this, there was something inexplicably captivating about the corners of her eyes and brows.

Sui Yuanqing stared at that beautiful yet fierce face and froze for the briefest instant.

That instant was enough.

The torch in his hand was sliced clean through, and the bloodletting knife plunged viciously into his shoulder blade. If she twisted and levered it upward with any more force, she might well tear his entire arm clean off.

The agony snapped Sui Yuanqing back to his senses. Before Fan Changyu could wrench the blade upward and sever the tendons, he shouted, “If you kill me, neither you nor the people in that well will ever make it out alive.”

Fan Changyu's blade halted.

Sui Yuanqing immediately found himself a bargaining chip. “Let me go, and I'll spare you and everyone in the well.”

Fan Changyu barked, “Why should I believe you?”

Just then, a bandit outside discovered the horse Sui Yuanqing had left at the mouth of the alley and called out: “**Wu Dangjia**, are *you* in there?”
<div class="author-note">
Dangjia (当家) refers to a leader or boss within a bandit stronghold. Fifth here refer to the rank of hierarchy between the leaders. Wu Dangjia means Fifth Chief.
</div>

The blood pouring from Sui Yuanqing’s wound had soaked through half his clothes, yet he merely raised the corner of his lips, utterly unhurried. “Whether you believe me or not is up to you.”

Everything was decided in an instant. Before the others outside could find their way in, Fan Changyu pulled the bloodletting knife from where it had been buried in Sui Yuanqing’s shoulder blade and replaced it with a smaller boning knife, pressing it between the ribs on his left side.

A thrust there would pierce the heart.

Fan Changyu said in a low voice, “I told you last time that I’d show you how a New Year’s pig is bled. If you don’t behave, once I push this knife in, you’ll be the one seeing  the King of Hell before me.”

Keeping the knife against him, she hauled him to his feet. When she kicked aside the massive stone slab covering the well, the bandits searching the Fan family’s courtyard heard the commotion and came looking in the rear courtyard as well.

Several torches illuminated the small courtyard. The group saw Sui Yuanqing pinning a woman into the corner against the wall, while an abandoned door panel lay on the ground. It seemed the noise they had heard had merely been the door panel falling over.

What they did not know was that beneath that door panel lay the very bloodletting knife that had gouged a deep hole in Sui Yuanqing’s shoulder blade.

Sui Yuanqing’s injuries had yet to heal, and he wore a heavy cloak draped over his shoulders. At that moment, Fan Changyu’s entire figure was concealed within the cloak.

Seeing this, the bandits exchanged knowing looks and began to laugh.

One of them said, “We’ve interrupted **Wu Dangjia**’s good time.”

Sui Yuanqing merely laughed and cursed, “If you know that, then get out.”

As he took advantage of the situation to slip an arm around Fan Changyu’s lower back, she nudged the boning knife forward another fraction. The flesh over his chest was sliced open just slightly, the cold edge of the blade pressing against his skin. Sui Yuanqing finally restrained himself, letting his hand rest loosely against her clothing. At first glance, it looked as though he were simply holding her in his arms.

But he lowered his voice and teased, “If you’re going to use me as a hostage, how else am I supposed to get you out of here and help you keep the people in that well hidden?”

Fan Changyu said nothing, though she did not move the blade any farther forward.

She appeared to shyly lean against him, with most of her face concealed beneath his cloak. In truth, however, she was coldly holding the knife against his vitals.

Sui Yuanqing lowered his eyes to the only part of her that remained visible in the firelight: her lustrous pearly-white ear.

A maddening itch rose at the roots of his teeth. He almost wished he could bite her earlobe—better yet, bite hard enough to draw blood and leave a mark behind.

Just the thought of it inexplicably brought a strange sense of delight to his heart.

Twice now, he had fallen into this woman’s hands, and each time he had come away more grievously wounded than before.

Unexpectedly, he did not find himself angry. He was accustomed to women who obeyed him without question. This, by contrast, was strangely novel.

It was like taming a spirited horse. He liked the feeling of slowly bringing such a wild creature to heel.

Yet the image of her calling out to the man in the green ghost mask as he pulled her back from the edge of the building flashed through his mind again. The maddening itch at the roots of his teeth intensified. He lowered his head slightly and leaned close to her ear, “Why isn’t that masked man coming out to help you?”

Fan Changyu pushed the boning knife forward another fraction without hesitation.

Feeling the chill of the blade against his chest, Sui Yuanqing straightened up as a faint, mocking smile played on his lips.

To anyone watching, it looked as though he had merely whispered something flirtatious to the woman in his arms, and the bandits kept stealing lecherous glances beneath Sui Yuanqing’s cloak.

Unfortunately, Fan Changyu’s entire figure was concealed beneath the cloak, so all they could tell was that she seemed taller than the average woman. Beyond that, there was little they could make out.

Sui Yuanqing said, “Let’s go. **Da Ge** must be getting impatient.”

Using the cloak to conceal what she was doing, Fan Changyu pressed the boning knife against him and walked him outside, already making her calculations.

After that little commotion, hardly any of the bandits had noticed the well in the courtyard.

The dry well had originally been used as a cellar by the locals. There was a rope ladder inside, so once the bandits left, the people hiding in the well could simply climb out.

All she had to do was hold this man hostage and wait until Auntie Zhao and the others had taken Changning and escaped. Then she would have him lead her somewhere secluded, where she could deal with him alone. Escaping from him would be no difficult matter.

But… after the authorities in Jizhou had put down the unrest, their investigation had revealed that this man was a rebel from Chongzhou. So how had he ended up colluding with this band of mountain bandits?

The notices posted by the authorities had never disclosed Sui Yuanqing’s identity. Fan Changyu only knew him as a rebel; she had no idea that he was the Shizi of Changxin Prince.

Fan Changyu was still pondering the matter when a voice suddenly rang out from outside the alley, “**Da Dangjia**<sup class="footnote-ref" data-footnote="First Chief"></sup> is here!”

Fan Changyu calmly cast a sidelong glance at the First Chief the bandits had mentioned, only to see the man’s face darken as he directly lashed his whip toward her.

Knowing full well that she absolutely could not take that blow herself, Fan Changyu pulled the boning knife to the side at the very instant the scar-faced man swung his whip.

She did not drive the knife any deeper, but she widened the wound instead.

Sui Yuanqing understood the silent threat and the smile at the corner of his lips deepened. When the whip came down toward her, he caught it with his bare hand.

The force of the blow made the whip recoil against the back of his hand, and a welt immediately rose across his skin. Yet he acted as though he felt no pain, simply looking up at the scar-faced man on horseback. “What is **Da Ge** doing?”

The scar-faced man barked, “You’re planning to take this woman back to Qingfeng Stronghold?”

Sui Yuanqing replied carelessly, “It’s not every day I come across a woman I take a liking to.”

The scar-faced man simply threw a knife at Sui Yuanqing’s feet. “If you still want to marry Shisan Niang, then kill this woman.”
