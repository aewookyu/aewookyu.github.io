The weather was hardly pleasant today. Fine snow had been falling without pause, and a thin layer of fragmented ice had formed along the edges of the river.

Just as Fan Changyu swept a bandit clinging to the side of the boat into the water with a bamboo pole, the young man behind her suddenly cried out. Fan Changyu turned around to see a bandit had grabbed one of his legs and was hauling him toward the water with all his might.

Another bandit was climbing up over the stern. Fan Changyu had no hands to spare. Gritting her teeth, she kicked the water bandit back into the river, then thrust her bamboo pole behind her. The bandit clutching the young man's ankle barely had time to draw breath before she jabbed him straight into the water. The freezing river water flooded his mouth and nose, and he choked half to death.

The young man hurriedly scrambled back up. Half his foot had already been dragged into the water, and now his trouser leg, shoe, and sock were soaked through. The biting cold had turned his lips white, yet he still remembered to thank Fan Changyu. "Thank you, **Guniang**<sup class="footnote-ref" data-footnote="young lady"></sup>..."

His expression suddenly changed, and he shouted, "Look out!"

Fan Changyu instinctively swung the bamboo pole behind her to block. A scar-faced man leaped out of the water, a broad saber in his hands as he hacked viciously toward her. The bamboo pole in Fan Changyu's hands was sliced cleanly in two.

Seeing the blade almost upon her with no time to dodge, Fan Changyu could only lean backward as far as possible to avoid the vital areas, while thrusting out the sharpened end of the broken bamboo pole.

She had been prepared to die so long as she could take one of them down with her. The makeshift bamboo spear indeed pierced the scar-faced man, but the blow that should have landed on her left shoulder never came down.

A sharp whistle of wind split the air beside her ear. An arrow carrying tremendous force flew past, almost brushing her temple. Fan Changyu could even feel the airflow scraping painfully against her cheek.

Clang—!

The grating crack of metal made one's teeth ache. The arrow struck straight into the broad saber in the scar-faced man's hands. Sparks burst outward, and the arrowhead shattered from the force of the collision. The steel saber in the scar-faced man's hands cracked apart piece by piece, like shattered ice.

The arrows that followed rained down upon the bandits clinging to the sides of the boat.

Everyone present was stunned.

The scar-faced man reacted with lightning speed. He immediately drew the short blade at his waist and severed the length of sharpened bamboo embedded in his body, then plunged back into the water rather than remain aboard as a living target.

Fan Changyu looked toward the direction from which the arrows had come. She saw a cavalry unit thundering toward them, their riders all wearing the same heavy armor and drawing great bows. For a moment, she could not tell which of them had fired the arrow that had shattered the scar-faced man's steel saber.

She simply assumed that the authorities in Jizhou Prefectural Office had learned of the massacre in Qingping County and sent troops to suppress the bandits. A great weight lifted from her heart.

Most of the mountain bandits were little more than a rabble. They could put up a few moves with their swords and sabers, but against mounted government troops skilled with the bow, all they could do was flee for their lives.

Arrows fell upon the water like a swarm of locusts. The bandits were packed densely together, and before long, cries of agony rang out one after another as blood spread across the surface of the river.

Seeing that they could not make it ashore, and with the river water freezing to the bone, a group of bandits skilled in swimming simply began pushing Fan Changyu's boat toward the middle of the river from underwater.

When Fan Changyu realized that the wooden boat beneath her feet was getting farther and farther from the ferry crossing, she immediately sensed that something was terribly wrong.

Once they were out of range of the archers, she would be alone aboard the boat against a group of desperate, cornered bandits. It would only become harder for her to deal with them.

Worse still, the current in the middle of the river was swift. The boat barely needed any rowing at all before the current carried it rapidly downstream.

The soldiers on shore had evidently noticed this as well and stopped firing their arrows. Several soldiers who knew how to swim unfastened their heavy armor and plunged into the river.

The bandits who had pushed the boat away from the ferry crossing now surfaced from beneath the water, preparing to seize the boat again. Fan Changyu had no time to worry about the young man. She grabbed him by the collar and said, "Forgive me!"

Then she hurled him toward the soldiers in the water.

The young man went white with shock. He only had time to call out, "**Guniang**!" before his entire body traced a long arc through the air and plunged into the river.

He was clearly unable to swim. It took him a while to resurface, before he desperately thrashed at the bone-chilling water and shouted for help.

The soldiers who had swum over had to expend considerable effort dodging his flailing hands before they could seize him by the back of his neck and drag him toward shore.

From the riverbank, Xie Zheng watched the scene with his lips pressed into a tight line. He nocked another arrow to the great bow in his hands. His personal guards were about to tell him that the boat had drifted too far out, well beyond the range of their bows. But in the very next instant, white-fletched arrows exploded from his bowstring one after another. Far in the distance, beside the boat, bodies slowly rose to the surface of the river.

The cavalrymen on shore, most of whom were hopeless in the water, stared blankly for a moment before erupting into waves of cheers.

Xie Zheng's face, however, remained cold.

The boat had already reached the middle of the river and was being carried downstream by the current. His arrows could no longer reach the bandits hiding beneath the boat.

The rescued young man was blue-white with cold. Soaked through, he lay on the ground and spat out several mouthfuls of water. Once he had caught his breath, the first thing he did was turn to the surrounding soldiers and say, "Quick! Save the young lady on that boat!"

Xie Zheng gave him a cold glance, then yanked the reins and galloped down the road running along the river.

His personal guards realized that he intended to take the land route to catch up with the boat and hurriedly mounted their horses to follow.

<br><br>

Fan Changyu stood aboard the boat with half a bamboo pole in hand. After knocking down the last bandit attempting to climb aboard, the surface of the river fell quiet for a moment. It seemed that all the bandits hiding beneath the wooden boat had been killed.

She did not dare let her guard down. After watching the river carefully for a while and confirming that there were no ripples apart from those caused by the current, she went to the bow, picked up the wooden oar, and prepared to row toward shore.

She had only taken two strokes when a large splash suddenly erupted from the water below. A bandit surfaced immediately afterward. Fan Changyu was startled and instinctively swung the oar at him.

But the bandit was already dead. The scar-faced man had used the moment when she was occupied with the dead bandit to leap up alongside the boat. He slashed at Fan Changyu's neck with a dagger. She twisted aside and avoided the blade, but he still managed to carve a long gash across her arm.

Fan Changyu grunted from the pain and swung the oar backward, driving the handle hard into the spot on the scar-faced man's abdomen where she had previously stabbed him with the bamboo pole.

Although the scar-faced man had managed to get aboard, the fresh blow aggravated his abdominal wound. He staggered back a step and collapsed onto the wooden boat, a vein standing out at his temple from the pain.

Fan Changyu drew her boning knife and lunged at him. The scar-faced man's pupils contracted. He rolled away awkwardly, avoiding the blade, then retreated to the stern and tried to negotiate with Fan Changyu. "Heroine, neither of us wants to die here in the river and end up as fish food. Rather than keep fighting until we're both injured, why don't we call a temporary truce? Whatever debts or grievances we have, we can settle them another day. What do you say?"

Fan Changyu appeared to give the proposal some serious thought. In the end, she put away her knife. "Sure."

The scar-faced man seemed to breathe a sigh of relief, but he remained at the stern, not daring to relax his guard in the slightest.

Once they passed through that stretch of turbulent rapids, the river became calmer and the boat slowed down. The pursuing cavalry could already be seen along the bank.

Xie Zheng was riding at the very front. The official road stood on higher ground, and after gauging the distance from the riverbank to the boat in midstream, he lashed his warhorse twice. The horse shot forward, leaving even his personal guards behind. After galloping past the boat by some distance, he abandoned his horse and strode almost at a run toward the river, removing his armor as he went. Bare-chested, he plunged into the water.

The boat was drifting downstream. To swim across toward the middle of the river, he first had to run some distance downstream so that he could intercept the boat when he reached midstream.

Back aboard the boat, the scar-faced man was focused solely on the cavalry pursuing them, anxiety evident on his face. Being as considerate as ever, Fan Changyu said, "How about I give you the oar and let you row yourself?"

The scar-faced man hesitated, then nodded.

Fan Changyu picked up the oar from the bow and threw it toward him. At the same time, she hurled her boning knife as well. She herself grabbed the bamboo pole, now cut down to barely two chi<sup class="footnote-ref" data-footnote="1 chi ≈ 31-33 cm"></sup> in length, and thrust it straight at the scar-faced man.

Under the three-layered assault, the scar-faced man had no chance of dodging everything. He could only do his best to avoid the boning knife aimed straight at his throat, then reach out to intercept the sharpened bamboo thrusting toward him. The oar smashed into his face head-on, nearly breaking the bridge of his nose and drawing blood.

What he hadn't anticipated was that the bamboo pole in Fan Changyu's hands was a feint too.

Fan Changyu had picked up the bamboo pole with her dislocated arm, enduring pain that bored straight into her bones. She had already tried to set the joint herself, but she was no physician, and this was the first time she'd ever suffered such a serious injury. She couldn't be certain she had aligned the bones correctly.

After pushing the bone back into place, her left arm could move again, but every movement sent splintering pain through her bones. Naturally, she couldn't exert much strength with it.

When the scar-faced man reached out to intercept the bamboo pole, Fan Changyu seized his hair with her right hand and slammed his head into the water. In that instant, her gaze turned vicious.

The scar-faced man thrashed desperately, but the hand pressing down on the back of his head possessed astonishing strength. He simply couldn't manage to lift himself back up.

Freezing river water flooded his mouth and nose, almost choking its way into his lungs.

Only after the scar-faced man's struggles had weakened did Fan Changyu haul him up briefly by his hair. He coughed violently, stripped of his former menacing air, and begged, "Heroine, please spare my life. I'll work like an ox or horse for you from this day forth..."

Fan Changyu recalled the mourning hall draped in white cloth and her parents' bodies lying in their coffins. Her gaze turned icy. She shoved him back into the river. After a moment, she hauled him up again, her voice thick with hatred. "Were you the one who ambushed and killed a retired escort guard over a treasure map?"

The scar-faced man assumed she was after money and hurriedly confessed, "That treasure map was fake. The real one is already in the hands of Prince Changxin. But I've run Qingfeng Stronghold for many years, and Qingfeng Stronghold isn't my only hideout. I've stashed all my silver somewhere else. Heroine, please spare my life, and I'll hand over every last treasure I own..."

Fan Changyu said coldly, "A vile criminal like you deserves to live? How many people died in Qingping County last night? Now answer me: were you the one who killed that escort guard?"

The scar-faced man had spent many years roaming the Jianghu. He recognized the tone of someone seeking revenge and quickly replied, "It wasn't me. It was **Lao Di**<sup class="footnote-ref" data-footnote="lit. Old Third"></sup> who did it."

With her enemy right before her, the blood surged violently through Fan Changyu’s veins. Her grip on the scarred man’s hair was so tight that her knuckles turned white. "Your stronghold's Third Chief killed him?"

The car-faced man hurriedly replied, "Yes, yes, that's right."

Fan Changyu roared, "You're all *raccoon dogs from the same hill*<sup class="footnote-ref" data-footnote="an idiom similar to cut from the same cloth"></sup>! I'll kill you first, then hunt down your stronghold's Third Chief and avenge my parents!"

The scar-faced man shouted, "You're Ma Taiyuan's daughter? Ma Taiyuan was a eunuch! How could he possibly have descendants?"

Fan Changyu froze, "Who is Ma Taiyuan?"

The scar-faced man said, "He was the Chief Escort Master of the Four Seas Escort Agency<sup class="footnote-ref" data-footnote="四海镖局 (Sìhǎi biāojú)"></sup>. He was the one entrusted with transporting the treasure map back then. A little asking around should be enough for Heroin to learn his name."

He paused, then added, "Could it be that Heroine is seeking vengeance against the wrong person?"

The authorities had clearly said that her own father was the one who transported the treasure map back then. How had Ma Taiyuan suddenly entered the picture?

Fan Changyu's mind was awash with questions. She demanded, "The Fan couple who died at Hucha Pass in Qingping County last year’s Eleventh Month—weren't they killed by people from Qingfeng Stronghold?"

The scar-faced man cried out in protest. "The only job our brothers pulled off in Qingping County was the one last night! We never went to Qingping County to kill anyone before that!"

Suspecting he was lying to save his own life, Fan Changyu shoved him back into the river. "Tell the truth!"

The scar-faced man thrashed until he was nearly spent. When she hauled him up again, his face had turned blue and purple, and the bitterly cold water made it difficult even to open his eyes.

"I'm telling the truth! Last year’s Eleventh Month, our stronghold was busy contending with Heilong Stronghold<sup class="footnote-ref" data-footnote="lit. Black Dragon Stronghold"></sup>. We weren't out robbing anyone. If Heroine doesn't believe me, ask around on the Jianghu roads!"

Fan Changyu was genuinely bewildered now. If that was the case, then the group of black-clad men who had attacked her home several times back then hadn't been bandits after all?

She had now truly crossed hands with these mountain bandits. Recalling the martial arts of those black-clad men, she was reluctant to admit it, but every one of them had been highly skilled. They really hadn't seemed like ordinary bandits.

Then what exactly had caused her parents' deaths?

The biting cold and the violent upheaval of emotions made Fan Changyu's head throb as though it were splitting apart.

The hand gripping the scar-faced man loosened slightly. Seizing the opportunity, he reached both hands backward, grabbed Fan Changyu by the arms, and yanked her into the river.

Her dislocated left arm sent piercing pain through her at the slightest contact. Caught off guard, Fan Changyu was thrown into the water and choked on a mouthful before she could react.

The scar-faced man had endured her humiliating him for so long, yet he did not immediately try to take her life. Instead, his face twisted with malice as he pressed her head beneath the water.

Whenever Fan Changyu was on the verge of losing the strength to struggle, he hauled her back up, only to repeat the process.

"You stinking bitch! Where'd that strength go—the strength you used to shove me underwater just now? Why aren't you struggling anymore?"

His face was alight with vengeful satisfaction.

Fan Changyu truly had no strength left. Desperate for air, she tried to breathe even though she was underwater. A long string of bubbles escaped from her mouth and nose as freezing water flooded into her chest, bringing agonizing pain.

Her eyes burned with pain as well. She knew she might die here.

But what would happen to Changning?

Changning...

A moment before she lost consciousness, Fan Changyu seemed to hear the sharp crack of something being forced out of joint. Then the hand gripping her hair suddenly released its hold.

Something warm and soft pressed against her lips. To someone about to die in the freezing river, that small warmth felt like the last comfort this world had to offer.

At last, she let her eyes fall heavily shut.

After blowing a breath of air into Fan Changyu's lungs, Xie Zheng hurriedly carried her up to the surface.

The scar-faced man's corpse floated sideways not far away. His head had been brutally twisted halfway around, leaving his neck grotesquely contorted. Terror still filled his eyes, which had remained open until the very end.

When the approaching personal guards saw that Xie Zheng had actually gone into the water himself to rescue someone, they hurriedly waded over to help.

By then, Xie Zheng had already swum with Fan Changyu to the shallows. Without saying a word, he carried her toward the bank, his face darker than any of them had ever seen. The muscles and veins stood out along his dripping arms, and the murderous aura radiating from him made it difficult for anyone nearby to breathe.

One of the guards came forward with their clothes. He had intended to call out to Xie Zheng, but instinctively fell silent. Remembering how their master had always forbidden women from approaching him, he instead said, "**Houyé**, let me carry this young lady."

Xie Zheng ignored the guard's outstretched hands. He simply snatched off his own cloak, wrapped it around Fan Changyu's soaked body, and continued walking with her in his arms.

The guard and several of his companions stood frozen on the spot. Before they could recover, they heard Xie Zheng say coldly, with a murderous edge to his voice, "Bring that bandit chief's corpse back. Lash it."

Even the personal guards, who could hack through men on the battlefield as easily as chopping melons, felt a chill shoot up their spines at those words.
