In the dense woods of Banpo hillside on the outskirts of Qingping County, several scouts trampled through dry grass where the last patches of snow had yet to melt, hurrying toward the army concealed among the pine trees.

“**Jiangjun**<sup class="footnote-ref" data-footnote="general"></sup>! A contingent of imperial troops is heading toward Qingping County!”

The young Chongzhou officer stationed here on standby was overjoyed at the news. “Are they flying the Wei banner?”

The scout replied, “We saw no Wei banner. They’re flying the Jizhou Prefecture banner.”

The expression on the young officer’s face grew difficult to read. He asked, “Who commands them?”

“An old general and a young commander.”

The young officer muttered, “Could it be Wei Xuan and He Jingyuan together?”

One of his men asked, “**Jiangjun**, do we still ambush those rioters surrounding Qingping County?”

The young officer shook his head. “The Jizhou Prefecture troops have arrived. Have our men lead the rioters in continuing their unrest. Better yet, have them storm the county seat. That way, no matter who has come from Jizhou Prefectural Office, those troops will have no choice but to engage the rioters.”

Once the rebellious county residents entered the city, the greater the civilian casualties within its walls, the more charges of wrongdoing could be laid at the Wei faction’s door.

Their Shizi<sup class="footnote-ref" data-footnote="the heir apparent to a noble household"></sup>’s original plan had been to withhold the military provisions collected from Qingping County. Given Wei Xuan’s temperament, he would inevitably fly into a rage and personally lead troops to seize the grain. When he encountered the rioting county residents, whose fury had already reached its peak, it would be like putting two firecrackers together. There would be no need to worry about a fight breaking out.

Once word spread that the imperial court had forcibly requisitioned grain and driven an entire county into rioting, and that the army had slaughtered unarmed civilians, the resulting uproar would be enormous.

<br><br>

The situation at the city gates was hardly optimistic.

Qingping County was merely a small county seat, and its military defenses had never received much attention. Even its rammed-earth walls were absurdly low. Aside from a bare gate tower, it had none of the usual defensive structures—not a barbican, an arrow tower, nor a projecting defensive wall.
<div class="author-note">
  <p>瓮城 (barbican): An enclosed defensive area outside or around the main gate, designed to trap and expose attackers.</p>
  <p>箭楼 (arrow tower): A fortified tower equipped for archers to defend the city.</p>
  <p>马面墙 (projecting defensive wall): A section of wall projecting outward from the main city wall, allowing defenders to attack enemies approaching along the wall’s sides.</p>
</div>

Having received advance warning, Head Constable Wang brought a group of yamen runners under his command to close the city gates, then hastily gathered a few bows and set them up in the arrow slits above the gate. Even so, the defenses looked pitifully sparse. There were not even enough men to fill out the length of the walls.
<div class="author-note">
瞭口 — Arrow slits / defensive openings. It refers to openings in a defensive structure through which defenders can observe the outside and potentially shoot.
</div>

Having a group of constables serve as the city’s entire defensive force was absurd enough in itself.

But Qingping County had no permanent military garrison. In the past several decades, apart from bandit raids, it had never experienced the ravages of war.

The farmers blocked beneath the city gate tower formed a dark, sprawling mass as far as the eye could see. Every one of them held a hoe or a rake, and the simple, honest faces they had once worn were gone. Now, each looked ferocious enough to tear the constables standing atop the gate tower limb from limb and devour them alive.

Even the young constables stationed on the city gate tower were frightened. Head Constable Wang felt a chill of fear at the sight. Thousands of farmers had gathered here—if they truly stormed the city, what could this little gate tower possibly hold them back with?

For now, Head Constable Wang could only hope that the Jizhou Prefectural Office had caught wind of the situation and would hurry troops over.

Remembering Fan Changyu’s instructions, he stood at an arrow slit and called down to the common folk in a conciliatory tone, “Fellow folks, what are you doing? Don’t let a moment of folly lead you to commit a crime that will bring extermination upon your entire clan!”

Most of the farmers who had followed the crowd this far were still wary of the bows above them and dared not press forward. Though they vastly outnumbered the defenders, no one wanted to be the first to throw his life away.

Everyone knew what crime rebellion constituted. Knowing it in their own hearts was one thing; hearing someone else admonish them about it was another.

Most of them had spent their entire lives eking out a living from their fields. Many had never even left Qingping County. As far as they knew, the greatest powers in the world were the officials and the Emperor, while the most powerful official in Qingping County was the county magistrate.

Crossing the county magistrate meant getting beaten with the cangue or thrown into prison. Crossing the Emperor meant that every relative within nine generations would be sent to the executioner’s block.

Even on ordinary days, they were terrified whenever they encountered these constables. Hearing Head Constable Wang say this now, they could not help but grow uneasy.

Seeing this, the man at the head of the crowd hardened his gaze and cursed at Head Constable Wang atop the walls, “You dog officials! When you were throwing your weight around, we farmers were nothing but lowly peasants for you to order around as you pleased. Now that you’ve driven us to the brink of starvation, we’re your ‘folks’ again? *Pei*<sup class="footnote-ref" data-footnote="spatting"></sup>! I’ll be damned if I’ll accept being called ‘folks’ by a county magistrate’s lapdog like you!

“Extermination of nine clans? We’ve lost even our seed grain! We won’t need the Emperor to wipe out our clan’s nine generations—we’ll starve to death first! If we’re going to die either way, we might as well storm the city, seize whatever money we can, and defect to the rebel prince of Chongzhou. At least then, there’ll still be a way to survive!”

The farmers who had wavered moments earlier grew resolute upon hearing his words. They shouted in unison, “The authorities won’t let us live! We’ll carve out a way to survive for ourselves!”

The leader raised his farming tool high. “Bring that dog magistrate out here to die!”

The farmers behind him joined in, shouting, “Bring that dog magistrate out here to face his death!”

Head Constable Wang could see that the situation was slipping beyond control. He hurriedly called down, “Folks, please remain calm and not act rushly! The seed grain… it will be returned to you all. Go home, each and every one of you. The authorities will not pursue this matter of rebellion any further.”

The man at the head of the crowd sneered. “Did you all hear that? When we weren’t rebelling, these dog officials didn’t give a damn about our lives. They beat people to death just to seize our seed grain. Now that we’ve risen up, they’re willing to return it! All the suffering we’ve endured, all the hardships we’ve borne these years—it’s only because they thought we were easy to bully!”

His words stoked the farmers’ resentment even further.

Seizing the momentum, the leader shouted, “We can’t retreat! The moment we do, these dog officials will be throwing their weight around again! And the wealthy families in this city—aren’t they all a bunch of snobs who look down on anyone beneath them? Whenever we came into town for market, they looked at us as if we were filth!

“Storm the city! Slaughter the dog officials, seize their gold and silver, and reclaim all the dignity we were forced to swallow!”

He shot a glance at several men behind him. Understanding his meaning, they joined in the shouting:

“Exactly! We weren’t born lowly! We just weren’t as fortunate as these city folk when it came to where we were born!”

“Folks, don’t let this county magistrate’s lapdog deceive you! He’s coaxing us to go home, but what awaits us then is the same fate as Majia Village!”

“We’ve come this far—what’s there to retreat from now? I’d rather die having lived *a life of pleasure*<sup class="footnote-ref" data-footnote="风流 can refer to romantic indulgence or sexual pleasure"></sup>! I hear the women in the city are so soft you could pinch water from their skin! Their flesh is as white as dough! Brothers who haven’t managed to find yourselves wives, don’t you want to be the overnight bridegrooms of some wealthy gentleman’s daughters?”

With the massacre at Majia Village still fresh in everyone’s minds, no one dared retreat. And with so many temptations waiting inside the city, the farmers behind him were practically red-eyed with excitement. Breathing heavily in the muddy ground, they shouted: “Storm the city!”

It was only after Head Constable Wang arrived at the city gates that he learned what had driven these farmers to rebellion. First, the government troops sent to requisition grain had been brutal and overbearing, treating the farmers as less than human. Second, when the people of Majia Village tried to travel to Jizhou Prefectural Office to bring the matter to wider attention, someone had slaughtered every last person in the village along the way.

He no longer even held the position of head constable, and thus lacked the confidence to promise that the seed grain would be returned. Now, seeing the rebellious farmers’ faces twisted into savage expressions like wild beasts, he could only plead earnestly, “Folks, don’t be foolish, don’t be foolish! Qingping County is so small! If you rebel here, do you truly think you’ll be able to escape all the way to Chongzhou? And even if you manage to escape, can your wives, children, and elderly mothers do the same?”

The ones shouting the loudest were mostly those from the countryside who had neither elders to support nor young children to raise.

When Head Constable Wang’s words struck home, the farmers causing the disturbance wore a variety of expressions.

Some of the farmers had only joined the unrest because they had been driven into a corner and had nowhere else to turn. One of them called out, “You said you’d return our seed grain. Does that promise still stand?”

Head Constable Wang was honestly unsure whether the authorities would actually return the grain. After hesitating for a moment, he gritted his teeth and said, “Of course it does!”

Those with relatives in Majia Village called out bitterly, “Hand over the dog officials and soldiers who slaughtered everyone in Majia Village and let them pay with their lives! Otherwise, this matter isn’t over!”

Head Constable Wang hurriedly said, “The authorities will certainly investigate the Majia Village massacre thoroughly and severely punish those responsible. We will give you all an explanation.”

Seeing that a few words from Head Constable Wang had begun to split the rebels into different factions, the men at the head of the disturbance exchanged a glance.

The man who had been shouting the loudest earlier continued to stir up the crowd. “Investigate thoroughly? Isn’t it still you dog officials who get to decide how the investigation is conducted? What’s to stop you from turning around and saying bandits killed them? What could we do then?”

It was hardly an impossible scenario. The crowd, which had begun to settle down, grew agitated once more. “Exactly! Hand over those dog-bastard soldiers right now!”

As they shouted, a group of them began pressing toward the city gates.

Head Constable Wang shouted, “Do not come any closer! Anyone who takes another step will be shot!”

The constables beside him drew their bowstrings taut, but their hands gripping the arrows trembled faintly.

The people below cursed even more viciously. “That Wang fellow is the county yamen’s head constable! For all we know, the ones who slaughtered Majia Village were his own men! How could he possibly hand them over?”

The farmers who had joined the rebellion were further inflamed by the jeers around them. Their anger burned hotter, and the looks they directed at Head Constable Wang grew even more hostile.

Head Constable Wang was already at his wits’ end when a commotion arose behind him. The newly appointed yamen runners came up onto the city wall, their faces dark with displeasure. One of them shoved the others aside and said with a grim expression on his fleshy face, “A bunch of dismissed men like you don't fit to wear these uniforms!”

Head Constable Wang and the constables below him flushed with humiliation and anger, their faces shifting between red and pale.

One of the men leading the crowd below spotted the newly arrived yamen runners, and a look of triumph flashed in his eyes. He shouted, “When have these dog officials ever treated our lives like they were worth anything? Let them shoot! If they kill me, folks, just don’t forget to avenge me!”

After shouting this, he strode forward. The “yamen runners” on the city wall seized the bows and arrows from the others and loosed a volley at the crowd below.

The few men who had been shouting the loudest were not struck by a single arrow. Instead, it was an ordinary farmer, driven forward by their incitement, who was killed by one of the arrows.

Once someone died, the uproar beneath the city wall grew louder still.

Someone who knew him wailed, “Er Dan!”

The men fanning the flames continued, “Everyone saw that! These government lapdogs never intended to let us live from the very beginning! Storm the city and fight them to the death!”

The man clutching the farmer who had been shot was evidently his brother. He immediately snarled, “I’ll fight you dog officials to the death!”

The farmers, their reason utterly consumed by rage, were about to throw caution to the wind and break through the city gate when suddenly—

Bang!

A tremendous impact rang out, and blood and flesh sprayed through the air beneath the city wall.

The farmers stared at the yamen runner who had fallen to his death beneath the city wall. They looked at one another, their advance coming to a halt, before raising their eyes to the city wall once more.

A man wearing a green demon mask stood atop the wall and said coldly, “Find whoever loosed the arrow and settle the score with him.”

The mask had been everywhere at the Lantern Festival on New Year’s Day. Yet worn upon his face now, it carried an indescribably eerie, sinister air.

The man leading the unrest felt an inexplicable stir of panic and demanded, “Who are you?”

Xie Zheng replied, “A man who kills corrupt officials.”

The real and fake yamen runners on the city wall finally came back to their senses as well. Head Constable Wang and the others had no idea what was happening, while the fake yamen runners drew their swords and charged at Xie Zheng.

Xie Zheng did not even bother to fight back. The cold wind billowed through his broad sleeves as he stood atop the city wall, his robes fluttering in the breeze. Whenever a sword came swinging at him, he simply sidestepped it, then seized the attacker by the collar and tossed him over the wall.

Another body hit the ground below.

While Head Constable Wang stood there in a daze, Xie Zheng casually flung another fake yamen runner from the wall with a sweep of his hand, then turned his head toward him and said, “The county magistrate has been placed under guard. These are all impostors. Tell your men to deal with them.”

Head Constable Wang came to his senses. Though he had no idea who the man wearing the green demon mask was, recalling all the strange happenings at the county yamen these past few days, he immediately understood what was going on. He hurriedly ordered the yamen runners under his command, “Seize these impostors!”

The constables who had no idea what was happening saw their chief charge forward and, unable to concern themselves with anything else, drew their swords and clashed with the fake yamen runners.

The farmers below craned their necks upward, watching the spectacle unfold like they were watching a grand show, their faces filled with confusion. “How come those constables are fighting among themselves?”

A farmer beside him answered, “Looks like Head Constable Wang’s men are fighting the constables who were shooting the arrows.”

“The county magistrate’s lot aren’t exactly good people, but Head Constable Wang’s a decent man. Once, our family’s ox wandered into the neighboring village, and that Chen **Laizi** from over there took it for himself. It was Head Constable Wang who went and got it back for us.”
<div class="author-note">
癞子 (làizi) is a rustic, insulting nickname for someone with ringworm or sores/scalp disease.
</div>

Seeing the situation slipping out of control, the instigators continued to fan the flames. “Can Head Constable Wang really overrule the county magistrate? These lapdogs are willing to kill even their former colleagues just to save their own skins. What value do you think our lives have in their eyes? If you want revenge, then we still have to break through these city gates and kill the county magistrate!”

Many of the farmers were clearly wavering, unsure whether they should simply enter the city or wait for the authorities to give them an explanation.

Before long, Xie Zheng and the others had thrown all the fake yamen runners from the city wall. The farmers who had never killed anyone before still felt a chill in their hearts at the sight of all those corpses strewn across the ground before the city gate.

Xie Zheng stood atop the city wall with his hands clasped behind his back. “Those willing to take their grain and go home may consider today’s events settled. The authorities will not pursue the matter any further. As for those who refuse to see reason, the Jizhou Prefecture army is already on its way to Qingping County. If you break through these city gates today and your hands are stained with even a single life, there will be no turning back. For the rest of your lives, do you want to keep farming and live peacefully with your wives, children, and parents, or drag your whole family to their deaths? The choice is yours.”

The moment they heard that the Jizhou Prefecture army was coming, the farmers who had spent their entire lives tilling the land were still deeply afraid.

Mercy and intimidation together proved effective, after all. Compared to returning to the peace and stability of their old lives, storming into the city to loot it only to have their entire families executed by the government troops was plainly a choice no sane man would make.

The instigators tried to make things difficult for him. “Words are cheap. Where’s the grain?”

Head Constable Wang was just about to back him up when a voice suddenly rang out from inside the city wall. “The grain is here!”

It turned out to be the workers from Yixiang Restaurant, carrying sacks of grain up onto the city wall.

Under the circumstances, there was no way they could open the city gates, so some of the grain was lowered down from the wall in baskets.

Several farmers stepped forward and untied the sacks to inspect them. Their faces broke into wide grins, yet they could not help wiping at their eyes with their sleeves. “It’s grain. It really is our grain!”

Once word spread that their grain had been returned, the hearts of most of the farmers who had joined the unrest finally settled.

Head Constable Wang stepped forward and said quietly to Xie Zheng, “Brave hero, thank you for saving Qingping County from this calamity. But if we simply return the grain requisitioned for the army to these farmers, the **Junye**<sup class="footnote-ref" data-footnote="a respectful address for soldiers or military personnel, literally combining jun (military/army) with ye (sir/master)."></sup> in Jizhou Prefecture… the county yamen won’t be able to account for it!”

Xie Zheng said, “The county magistrate can account for it.”

The order abolishing the grain requisition had already been sent to Jizhou Prefecture together with the military order he had issued commanding Wei Xuan to return to Huizhou and hold the line. There was no way Jizhou Prefecture would continue requisitioning grain now. But for a constable who knew nothing of this, there was no need for him to explain all that.

Head Constable Wang had originally been at his wits’ end, but after hearing Xie Zheng’s words, he steeled himself.

Indeed, he had done all he could by appeasing these would-be rebels and preventing them from entering the county seat.

For an old man like him, this was all the responsibility he could shoulder. Anything beyond that, the county magistrate could bear himself.

He said, “The way you thought on your feet was truly impressive, young man. You even thought to use the Jizhou army to frighten these rebels. At least this spared the people inside the city from disaster.”

Xie Zheng said nothing. He had not actually invoked the arrival of the Jizhou Prefecture army merely to frighten the farmers below. An incident this serious in Qingping County—there was no way the Jizhou Prefectural Office had heard nothing of it.

So long as the one who came was not Wei Xuan, there was no chance the army would end up fighting these farmers who had been led around by the nose.

Seeing that the farmers who had joined the rebellion were gradually being pacified, the instigator, realizing that his hopes of high office and generous rewards were about to vanish, darkened his face and pressed the matter again. “What about the dozens of lives lost at Majia Village?”

Head Constable Wang looked to Xie Zheng for help.

The green demon mask concealed his entire face, making it impossible to tell what expression he wore. He merely said, “Buy time.”

Head Constable Wang was momentarily taken aback, but then understood. There really was no way to investigate the massacre at Majia Village and uncover anything conclusive right now, nor could they give these people an explanation on the spot.

They could only wait until the Jizhou Prefecture soldiers arrived and stabilized the situation before dealing with it.

He wiped the sweat from his temple and did his best to smooth things over with the troublemakers below the wall.

Xie Zheng’s gaze, meanwhile, fell unobtrusively upon the few men who had repeatedly provoked the crowd.

They were not seeking justice at all. They simply wanted to stir up the farmers’ hatred and make the situation as chaotic as possible.

But what did they stand to gain by making things worse?

The farmers whose *faces were always towards the yellow earth and back toward the sky*<sup class="footnote-ref" data-footnote="an expression for farmers who spend their lives bent over the fields, emphasizing their humble, hard-working existence."></sup> were not good with words. Led by their hatred and manipulated by these men, they had been goaded into committing wrongdoing. Those farmers would have no way of escaping the consequences.

For these men to be so utterly fearless, there had to be some backing behind them—and that backing was certainly worth looking into.

Just as the instigators seized upon the authorities’ inability to give an immediate explanation for the massacre at Majia Village and continued stirring up trouble, reigniting the farmers’ hatred toward the authorities, Xie Zheng was preparing to deal with those few men in secret when a voice suddenly rang out from the city wall: “The county magistrate arrives—!”

The people below the wall fell silent one after another, all of them turning hostile eyes toward the city wall.

Xie Zheng’s eyes narrowed as well. He assumed the people behind all this had forced the county magistrate to come forward and show himself. But when he turned for another glance, he saw the county magistrate strutting at the front, his prosperous belly preceding him, while a group of household servants followed behind, escorting bound soldiers and officials.

Fan Changyu wore an ill-fitting maidservant’s outfit, and she too had someone in custody, the bone-cleaving knife in her hand pressed against his neck. Because the sleeves were a little too short, half of her frost-white wrist was exposed.

Several shallow bloody cuts already marked the neck of the man she was holding, clearly the result of his being less than cooperative along the way.

Xie Zheng’s gaze fell upon the man’s face.

He froze.

Then, beneath the green demon mask, his expression became as colourful as it could be.
