Fan Changyu’s entire attention was focused on the man she had restrained. He was far too cunning. On the way here, he had deliberately kept talking to her, trying to distract her. Once, he had nearly tripped her and snatched the knife from her hand.

After that, Fan Changyu had become wary. She ignored everything he said, and whenever he pushed her too far, she would make a small cut somewhere on him as a warning.

By now they had reached the city gate tower. Fan Changyu only had time to hurriedly survey the situation below, and for a moment, she did not recognize that the man in the green demon mask was Xie Zheng.

Head Constable Wang stared at the pile of people they had tied up together like a string of zongzi, and was somewhat dumbfounded. He asked the County Magistrate, “**Daren**, this is…”

The County Magistrate was somewhat frightened when he saw the rebellious commoners below glaring at him with hatred. But then he thought about how Qingping County had managed to hold out, and how there was now someone for the people to vent their anger on. If he could claim credit before the Jizhou Prefectural Office for putting down the unrest in Qingping County, he might even have a chance at promotion. The thought was so heartening that the fat on his body stopped trembling.

He put on that inscrutable air so characteristic of officialdom and said, “The generals from Jizhou Prefectural Office were responsible for overseeing the grain requisition. Now that the people are seething with resentment, they must be given an explanation. **Ben Guan**<sup class="footnote-ref" data-footnote="lit. this official"></sup> therefore acted against my superiors and… ordered these **Junye**<sup class="footnote-ref" data-footnote="lit. soldier lord"></sup> to be bound.”

As he spoke, he even glanced at Fan Changyu. Once he confirmed that she had no intention of exposing the fact that he was actually the one who had been held captive, his expression became even more self-satisfied.

The County Magistrate’s household servants wore various expressions, but they had long since grown accustomed to playing the County Magistrate’s lackeys. If he could call white black, they would shut their eyes and agree. There was no way they would undermine him now.

Fan Changyu’s face showed not the slightest trace of anything unusual. To the County Magistrate’s people, this only made her seem like an honest, sensible young woman who neither fought for credit nor caused trouble.

Those like Head Constable Wang, who had absolutely no idea what was really going on, simply treated Fan Changyu as part of the background. All his attention remained fixed on the County Magistrate. Though he was still somewhat hesitant, the facts were right before his eyes. For the County Magistrate to have the nerve to tie up these soldiers showed a certain amount of resolve. He praised him, “**Daren** is truly righteous.”

The County Magistrate thought to himself that Head Constable Wang had brought men to guard the city gate, and that he was also the one who had captured the rebel ringleader. Once this matter was settled, when Jizhou Prefectural Office handed out rewards according to merit, Constable Wang would surely be credited with the greatest merit. If he wanted to take that credit for himself, he would have to flatter the man a little first.

So he immediately said, “The fact that the rioters still haven’t managed to enter the city is also thanks to you, Head Constable Wang, for bringing your men here to guard this place in advance. In order to make those soldiers and officials sent from Jizhou Prefectural Office lower their guard, I only pretended to dismiss you from your post. Head Constable Wang truly has not failed **Ben Guan**’s expectations.”

Head Constable Wang felt extremely guilty. He hurriedly said, “Wang Chuanxian<sup class="footnote-ref" data-footnote="this is his full name"></sup> is ashamed…”

He was just about to say that it had been Fan Changyu’s idea when he looked up and saw Fan Changyu frantically signalling at him.

Fan Changyu could not have been happier to let the County Magistrate take all the credit.

She wasn’t stupid. The man she had tied up was an official from Jizhou Prefectural Office. If he died, and that damned general above him—some Wei Xuan—heard her name, he would surely bear a grudge against her.

She was just an ordinary commoner. If she were thrust into the limelight, at most she would receive some gold, silver, and other rewards and become the most famous person in all of Qingping County. But the price would be earning the hatred of an official far more powerful than the County Magistrate. The other party could crush her with a single finger. Whether she would even live long enough to spend those rewards would be another matter entirely!

Besides, if this man in her grasp happened to be handed over to the people to vent their hatred on and somehow survived, he would surely hold a grudge against her too. Although she had already rather shamelessly hinted that she was acting on the County Magistrate’s orders, that timid, frightened-looking County Magistrate hardly seemed like the sort of person with the cunning or foresight to have plotted all this.

Now that the County Magistrate was greedily claiming the credit, he was telling the story with such convincing detail that he had successfully drawn all the hatred toward himself, much to her secret delight.

Head Constable Wang, meanwhile, was thoroughly confused. Seeing Fan Changyu signal for him to keep quiet, the rest of his sentence caught in his throat.

Just then, the commoners gathered below the city wall saw the County Magistrate arrive and put on that official’s airs. Countless people immediately grew furious and began shouting curses, “Corrupt dog of an official! How are you going to repay those dozen or so lives from Majia Village? With the lives of your entire family?”

The County Magistrate had never heard such vulgar language in his life. At this moment, his mind was already filled with thoughts of how he would rise rapidly through the ranks once he was promoted. So when the curses suddenly rang out, the rage made even the whiskers at the corners of his mouth tremble. “How dare you, insolent commoners! How dare you curse an appointed official of the imperial court!”

The public sentiment that Head Constable Wang and Xie Zheng had worked so hard to calm through a combination of mercy and intimidation immediately boiled over again because of that one sentence from the County Magistrate.

The troublemakers in the crowd seized the opportunity to fan the flames.

“Everyone saw it! This corrupt dog never regarded us as people in the first place, and he never intended to give us an explanation!”

“If we really let them fool us into going home like this, then tomorrow it’ll be that pack of yamen runners coming to our doors with clubs, ready to beat us to death!”

“Kill the County Magistrate! Demand justice!”

The farmers below the city gate tower surged with anger once more, raising their farm tools and shouting. Amid the roar of their voices, the little gatehouse of the county town was like a tiny boat on the sea; one crashing wave could reduce it to a heap of splintered wood.

Seeing the way things were turning, the County Magistrate inevitably panicked as well. He hurriedly ordered the household servants to bring Sui Yuanqing and the others forward. “I’m only a minor County Magistrate. How could I possibly have had any say over the grain requisition? The entire matter was overseen by the people sent from Jizhou Prefectural Office. As for what happened in Majia Village, **Ben Guan** knew nothing about it either! If you want justice, then **Ben Guan** has no choice but to *risk the censure of the whole world*, bind these men, and give you the justice you seek!”
<div class="author-note">
“冒天下之大不韪” — This idiom means to do something considered gravely unacceptable despite widespread opposition or condemnation, which is this case, defy the higher ups.
</div>

As he spoke, he ordered the men below, “Open the city gates. Send these people out!”

Xie Zheng’s gaze had remained quietly fixed on Sui Yuanqing all this time. When he saw the corners of Sui Yuanqing’s lips curl into a cold smile at those words, and noticed that several of the troublemakers mixed among the farmers were repeatedly glancing toward Sui Yuanqing, he said coldly, “We cannot let this man leave the city.”

Head Constable Wang hurriedly added, “**Daren**, we cannot open the gates! If we open them, all those rioters outside will swarm in, and the people inside the city will suffer for it.”

Fan Changyu only realized that the man in the mask was Xie Zheng when she heard his voice. She could not help looking up at him in surprise.

Hearing his voice as well, Sui Yuanqing frowned and began sizing up the man standing some distance away.

The County Magistrate and Head Constable Wang were still arguing when several sharp sounds suddenly cut through the air.

Several sleeve arrows shot out from the crowd, flying straight toward the County Magistrate and Fan Changyu atop the city gate tower.

Flying alongside those sleeve arrows were a group of death warriors dressed as farmers. They had flung *eagle-claw hooks*<sup class="footnote-ref" data-footnote="鹰爪钩 — A grappling hook with hooked claws used to catch onto walls or other elevated surfaces."></sup> up onto the crenellations of the rammed-earth city wall, then stepped on the heads of people below and hauled themselves rapidly up the ropes onto the tower.
<div class="author-note">
垛口 or “crenellations” - The gaps/openings along the top of a defensive wall through which defenders could observe or attack enemies.
</div>

Head Constable Wang was horrified. Drawing his saber, he shouted, “Protect **Daren**!”

Fan Changyu saw the sleeve arrow flying straight toward her face and instinctively turned her head to evade it. The man she had been restraining, however, suddenly threw himself against the boning knife in her hand. He twisted aside to avoid the vital point—his neck—and instead let his shoulder take the blade, dragging it heavily across the flesh and opening a bloody gash. The ropes binding him were severed as well.

By the time Fan Changyu realized what had happened, the man with yet another wound on his shoulder, blood streaming from it, was already flashing her a vicious, feral smile.

Sensing danger, she instinctively sprang backward, but Sui Yuanqing was faster. He tore free of the ropes, snatched the saber from a nearby yamen runner, and brought it crashing down toward Fan Changyu.

His killing technique had been honed on the battlefield through the taking of countless heads—ruthless and terrifyingly fast.

The boning knife in Fan Changyu’s hand was too short to give her an advantage against the long saber in his grasp. When she raised it to block, the sheer force of the blow left the web of her thumb and forefinger numb.

Xie Zheng intercepted the sleeve arrow flying toward Fan Changyu with one hand. But when he saw Sui Yuanqing break free and turn his blade on Fan Changyu, his expression changed, and he moved to go to her aid.

The death warriors who had used their eagle-claw hooks to scale the city wall seemed to see what he intended. While continuing to fire hidden arrows at Fan Changyu, several of them broke away to hold Xie Zheng back.

Xie Zheng had to shield Fan Changyu from the hidden arrows while dealing with these death warriors clinging to him like burrs. For a moment, he simply could not be in two places at once.

The yamen runners were utterly outmatched by the death warriors. Head Constable Wang’s men fell one after another, while the County Magistrate’s household servants, who had been guarding the prisoners, had never witnessed anything like this. They were nearly scared out of their wits and could think only of fleeing down from the city tower. Never mind helping anyone—they left themselves completely exposed and were cut down where they stood.

For a moment, Sui Yuanqing’s men actually gained the upper hand on the city gate tower.

Fan Changyu was forced to retreat again and again by the ferocity of Sui Yuanqing’s saber techniques. With her weapon a full head shorter than his, she could not afford to pull her strength, and the force of their blades colliding had already split the web of her hand, little beads of blood welling from the cracks.

She clenched her teeth against the pain. She knew that the reason she had managed to bind him so easily at the County Magistrate’s residence was because he had been completely off guard at the time, allowing her to seize the initiative from the very beginning.

But in this fight, every one of his moves went straight for a vital point, vicious and ruthless. Though she knew martial arts, she had little real combat experience, and she was nowhere near as underhanded as he was. With her weapon inferior as well, Fan Changyu was almost completely suppressed.

She wanted to grab a longer saber, too, but Sui Yuanqing’s blade came at her so relentlessly that she had no chance to spare even a moment to pick one up. She could only make do with the boning knife in her hand.

At last, she caught an opening and hurled the boning knife at him like a hidden weapon. Sui Yuanqing was forced to turn his head aside to evade it, and Fan Changyu immediately ducked down to grab the saber of a dead yamen runner lying on the ground.

But the saber in Sui Yuanqing’s hand seemed to have eyes of its own. The very next moment, it swept past her fingers. To save her hands, Fan Changyu had no choice but to abandon the saber. She threw herself into a roll across the ground, narrowly evading his second slash as it swept over her head.

The corners of Sui Yuanqing’s mouth curled high, his eyes gleaming with the amusement of a cat toying with a mouse. “You stabbed me so many times. Surely you’ll let me return the favor. Then I’ll skin you and hang you from the city gate. That would only be fair, wouldn’t you agree?”

Fan Changyu spat viciously to the side. “**Gu Nainai**<sup class="footnote-ref" data-footnote="lit. grand-aunt, but in this context it's like saying “this woman”, but in a highly-assertive first-person pronoun."></sup> just happened to leave my pig-killing knife at home today. Otherwise, I’d damn well show you how a New Year pig gets bled!”

Hearing the insult in her words, Sui Yuanqing’s expression darkened further. Whatever amusement he had taken in toying with her vanished in an instant. He advanced with his saber raised. “You’re courting death!”

Fan Changyu was just as reckless. Imitating what he had done earlier, she did not dodge. Instead, she charged straight toward the blade.

Xie Zheng caught sight of this from a distance. His phoenix eyes turned cold, and he snatched the saber from a death warrior before hurling it viciously at Sui Yuanqing.

The death warrior let out a scream—the bones in his hand had actually been snapped by Xie Zheng when he wrested the saber away.

As the cold blade came hurtling toward him, Sui Yuanqing’s pupils contracted. For the sake of his own safety, he had no choice but to change his stance and block the incoming saber.

The two blades collided with a shrill metallic clang, and the ring-pommel saber in Sui Yuanqing’s hand snapped cleanly in two.
<div class="author-note">
环首刀 — “ring-pommel saber”: A historical Chinese saber characterized by the metal ring at the end of its hilt. This is different than the generic 长刀  (long saber) used elsewhere in the fight.
</div>

Such tremendous force made him look up in astonishment at the man wearing the green demon mask.

He had thought the man’s voice sounded familiar when he heard him speak just now. And on the battlefield, there was only one person whose strength of hand he had ever experienced to such a degree. Could this man be…

The moment that person came to mind, Sui Yuanqing’s attention faltered—and Fan Changyu’s elbow slammed hard into his jaw. His entire body went flying backward. For quite some time afterward, his lower jaw was completely numb, and both rows of teeth felt as though the impact had knocked them loose. The taste of blood filled his mouth. The blow had probably rattled his cheekbones as well, because his ears were ringing, and for a moment he could barely make out the sounds around him.

Suddenly, he was no longer so certain that the masked man was Marquis Wu’an. Even an unremarkable young woman in Qingping County possessed such astonishing strength; who knew what other *crouching tigers and hidden dragons*<sup class="footnote-ref" data-footnote="卧虎藏龙 — referring to people of extraordinary ability who remain concealed or underestimated."></sup> might be lurking here…

Fan Changyu was not one to forget a grudge. Just moments ago, this man had bullied her while she had no proper weapon in hand, forcing her onto the defensive with a saber while she could only fend him off with a boning knife barely longer than a dagger. After driving her elbow into his jaw, she immediately picked up a ring-pommel saber lying on the ground and swung it at him once more.

With only a broken saber left in his hand, Sui Yuanqing’s expression hardened, and in the end, he chose to avoid Fan Changyu’s edge.

This time, it was Fan Changyu who swung her saber relentlessly without pause. Sui Yuanqing retreated as he dodged, and all along the way, the city walls on either side were left with saber marks a finger deep.

Mu Shi and several death warriors turned their heads and saw their Shizi being chased down by someone with a saber. They hurriedly disengaged and came over to help.

Suddenly, the chaotic sound of hoofbeats came from the official road in the distance. Looking up, they saw the Jizhou Prefecture banners snapping fiercely in the cold wind.

By the time the commoners gathered beneath the city gate had seen the fighting erupt atop the city gate tower, they could no longer tell what was happening. Those farmers dressed in coarse short jackets were all highly skilled fighters, yet not one of them was familiar to the crowd. With the situation descending into such chaos, and the few people who had been leading the troublemaking no longer there, the others were even less willing to act rashly.

Now that they saw the Jizhou army approaching, let alone risking a confrontation, they were even afraid the soldiers might mistake them for being in league with those highly skilled “farmers” atop the city gate tower. So they voluntarily cleared a path.

Taking advantage of several death soldiers surrounding Fan Changyu, Mu Shi helped Sui Yuanqing to his feet. Glancing at the approaching troops from Ji Prefecture, he urged, “**Shizi**, *so long as the green hills remain, there’ll be no shortage of firewood*<sup class="footnote-ref" data-footnote="A proverb meaning that as long as one survives, there will be opportunities to recover or try again."></sup>. Let’s withdraw first!”

Sui Yuanqing stared fixedly at Fan Changyu. Seeing that the dozen or so death warriors were no longer able to hold back the man in the green demon mask, he suddenly snatched Mu Shi’s saber and charged at the County Magistrate.

The County Magistrate shrieked in terror, while Head Constable Wang—already covered in several slashes—hurriedly threw himself forward to save him.

How could Fan Changyu stand by and watch Head Constable Wang die right before her eyes? Yan Zheng was also completely tied down by wave after wave of death warriors, leaving him unable to break free. She swung her saber around, intending to block the blow Sui Yuanqing was bringing down.

But Sui Yuanqing’s attack was only a feint. He abandoned the saber outright, his hand winding around Fan Changyu’s weapon-wielding wrist like a vine. Somehow, with a deft application of force, he made Fan Changyu feel as though her entire arm had instantly gone numb. The saber fell from her hand with a resounding clang.

“I’ve changed my mind. It’d be a shame to skin you and hang that fine hide of yours on the city wall. Come back with me and be my concubine instead.”

Sui Yuanqing gripped the rope attached to the eagle-claw hook with one hand and held fast to Fan Changyu with the other. Laughing uproariously, he leapt from the *parapet* where a section had collapsed.
<div class="author-note">
女墙 — Literally “woman wall”. It refers to the low protective wall along the edge of a city wall or tower, the low crenellated protective wall where defenders could take cover.
</div>

The sudden pull sent Fan Changyu stumbling. Before she could steady herself, she was dragged over the edge with him. Instinctively, she cried, “Yan Zheng!”

At the critical moment, another large hand, its sinews and bones sharply defined, shot out from the city gate tower and seized Fan Changyu firmly by the arm.

Even behind the mask, the aura emanating from Xie Zheng was frighteningly cold and murderous at that moment. He swung the long-handled saber in his hand straight at the hand Sui Yuanqing had wrapped around Fan Changyu. The force behind the blow was so savage that there was no doubt the arm would be severed cleanly in the next instant.

Sui Yuanqing had no choice but to grit his teeth and release his hold on Fan Changyu. Even so, the fierce blade wind sliced off a strand of hair beside his temple and left a shallow cut across his face.

Sui Yuanqing looked up and met the vicious gaze beneath the green demon mask. A flicker of alarm stirred in his heart.

With Xie Zheng gripping her other arm, Fan Changyu finally had something solid to brace against. Without the slightest hesitation, she kicked straight at Sui Yuanqing’s face, while ordering Xie Zheng, “Quick, quick! Cut the rope! Let that bastard fall to his death!”

The eagle-claw hook anchoring the rope to the city wall’s crenellation snapped with a sharp crack. But as Sui Yuanqing fell, he kicked off the wall several times to break his descent, while a group of his personal guards seized the rope and hauled him back. He landed without a scratch, except for a dark footprint smeared across half of his handsome face.

Fan Changyu was inevitably sorely disappointed when she saw this. Even as Xie Zheng hauled her back up, she was still grumbling with lingering resentment, “How did that bastard not fall to his death…”

The very next moment, however, she was enveloped in a broad, solid chest. The force was so great that she felt as though she had been clamped against a slab of iron, and her muttering abruptly came to a halt.
